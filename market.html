When a buyer pays online, NewMart holds the money securely — the seller cannot touch it. The seller prepares and ships the order, the buyer confirms delivery, and only then is the payment released, minus our 5% commission. If anything goes wrong, the buyer raises a dispute and our team steps in to refund or resolve it.
