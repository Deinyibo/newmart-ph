Questions, answered

The buyer pays NewMart, not you directly. The money is held safely while you prepare and ship the order. Once the buyer confirms delivery, it is released to your wallet minus 5%. If there is a dispute, the money stays put until it is resolved — so buyers trust you faster.

Yes — POD is a first-class option on NewMart. You deliver, you collect cash or transfer on the spot, and NewMart invoices you the 5% commission monthly. New buyers may have a POD value limit for everyone's safety.
